# Chat

*Only on boxes installed with the **full** profile, and only once an AI model is on the box
(add one under [Content](content.html)).*

The **Chat** tile (<https://chat.deck>) lets you ask the box's AI questions in plain English
— "how do I treat a burn?", "what does git rebase do?".

## Wiki first

You don't need to say "look it up": for every question the box first looks in its own
**wiki**, reads the best articles and answers from them. Such answers end with
**Sources** — links to the articles, so you can read more and check.

If the wiki has nothing on it, the answer starts with:

> Not found in the wiki — this answer comes from the model's own training and may not be
> precise.

Treat those answers with care, especially for health or safety questions.

## Waiting

This computer has no graphics card, so answers take time — often a minute or more. While
you wait, the page shows what the box is doing (looking at the book map, searching,
reading, writing). You can close the page and come back later: the answer is written
anyway and shows up when you return.

Only one answer is written at a time. If someone else is asking too, your question waits
("Waiting — another answer is being written"); you can cancel it while it waits.

## Chats

Every conversation is kept on the box (left side). Follow-up questions remember what was
said before. Rename or delete a chat at the top. There are no user accounts: everyone at
this box sees the same chats.
